Red Velvet is a 5-member South Korean girl group that debuted under SM Entertainment on August 1, 2014. Originally they debuted with 4 members (Irene, Seulgi, Wendy, and Joy), but in March 2015 the fifth and final member, Yeri, joined the group.

ENHYPEN

[include picture of enhypen above]

ENHYPEN is a 7-member (in my heart) South Korean boy group that debuted under Belift Lab on November 30, 2020. Originally they debuted with 7 members (Heeseung, Jay, Jake, Sunghoon, Sunoo, Jungwon, and Ni-ki). On March 10, 2026 Heeseung (now known as Evan) left the group to pursue solo activities.

Rating: i LUV them <3

IVE

[include picture of ive above]

IVE is a 6-member South Korean girl group that debuted under Starship Entertainment on December 1, 2021. They debuted with 6 members (Gaeul, Yujin, Rei, Wonyoung, Liz, and Leeseo).
